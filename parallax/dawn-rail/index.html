Dawn Rail is an original procedural pixel-art graphics demo. Move horizontally to shift the seat and camera. Drag vertically or use Up and Down arrows to change the window shade and exposure. Click or press Space to clear circular condensation. Press Q to toggle reflection and bird quality, P to compare night, sunrise, and green-day palettes, and R to reset the sixty-second journey. The animation pauses while this tab is hidden and respects reduced-motion preferences.
